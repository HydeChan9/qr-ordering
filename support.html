Quote FAQ

Do I need artwork before requesting a quote?

No. A keyboard reference, desk photo, colour direction, or written idea is enough to begin.

When do I pay?

No payment is taken with this form. Scope, proof, availability, timing, and price are confirmed first.

Can you match an exact colour from my screen?

Screen colours vary. Final colour expectations and available sample or proof options are discussed before production.

What if a third-party part has a fault?

Contact ForgeKeys AU first for goods or services supplied by us. We assess the issue and may coordinate with the supplier or manufacturer. This process does not replace your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

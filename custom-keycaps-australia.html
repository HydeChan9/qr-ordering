What to send

Four simple pieces are enough to begin.

JPG, PNG, or WebP images are accepted. Add up to three files, 3 MB each, or start with a written idea only.

  1. Your ideaA photo, artwork, logo, palette, or a few words about the theme.
  2. Your keyboardThe model or layout, such as 75%, TKL, Alice, or full size.
  3. Your prioritiesWhich keys matter most and whether the design should be subtle or bold.
  4. Your budgetA comfortable range helps us recommend a realistic direction.

Common questions

Start with the idea. We will check the practical details.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes. Send the clearest version you have. We will review cropping and resolution, then request the original high-resolution file if the idea moves forward.

Do I need artwork before requesting a quote?

No. A theme, colour direction, desk photo, or written idea is enough for the first enquiry.

Can you quote one key or only a full set?

You can enquire about one novelty key, a group of accents, large keys, or a complete layout. Feasibility and minimum order depend on the selected production route.

Can the 3D preview go straight to a factory?

No. Production templates, safe areas, legends, material, colour, and print method are checked after proof and quote approval.

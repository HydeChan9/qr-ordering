Before you enquire

Common custom build questions.

Can I ask for recommendations without knowing keyboard parts?

Yes. Start with your layout, budget, desk photo, and the feel or sound you want. We can suggest a practical direction before a final scope is prepared.

Can ForgeKeys AU source the parts?

Sourcing can be included when suitable parts are available. The quote identifies what ForgeKeys AU supplies, what you supply, and the relevant warranty route.

Can I provide my own keyboard kit or parts?

Yes, subject to compatibility and condition review. Customer-provided parts are listed separately in the written scope.

Is the 3D preview required?

No. It is an optional visual tool. A normal quote can begin with text and an optional image.

Sydney service questions

Before bringing in a keyboard.

Is there a Sydney walk-in store?

No public walk-in location is advertised. Delivery or handover options are discussed privately and confirmed in the written quote.

Can I send only a keyboard model and budget?

Yes. Add what you want to change about the feel, sound, or appearance, and we can identify the questions needed for a practical scope.

Should I bring or ship the keyboard before receiving a quote?

No. Start with the enquiry. Do not move hardware until the scope, inspection needs, and handover or delivery arrangement are confirmed.

Can the service include parts sourcing?

It can when suitable parts are available. The quote separates ForgeKeys-supplied items, customer-provided items, labour, and the relevant warranty route.

How an order works

02

Fit check

Submit keyboard model, layout, key sizes, and production notes so the concept can be checked.

03

Proof

Receive a visual proof, material recommendation, quote, and estimated production timing.

04

Quote payment

After the proof and quote are approved, pay the deposit or final balance with your ForgeKeys quote reference.
